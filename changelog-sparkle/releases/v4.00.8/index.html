Dock Exposé 4.00.8 release notes

Changes since v4.00.7:

  • Add a Repeat Delay slider for hold-to-repeat arrow switching
  • Hold-to-repeat for Control+Left/Right space switching
  • target the display under the mouse
  • queue instead of dropping during a switch, resolve on the tap thread
  • flag currentlySwitchingSpaces, CGSSpaceID nextSpaceSwitchTarget (schedule next space after current switch finishes)
  • stop running multiple [spaces refresh] at a time
  • ] nudge space or go to space ±1 and ctrl+left/right (fixes glitch where CGSCopyManagedDisplayForSpace causes current space windows to join destination space until nudge (and nudge doesn't work for 1st and last spact in monitor so we do ±1 trick))
  • Add space digit shortcuts and group preferences into sections
  • don't re-open after a scroll-to-close
  • don't re-open after a user Escape
  • bump version
