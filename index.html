Introduction

Dock Exposé is a window and task management app for macOS 26 and 27. It provides Dock window previews using the familiar App Exposé interface and adds close/minimize controls, window-title labels, and a custom editable bottom bar for recent documents and minimized windows. Press fn+R to open the Recents Editor at any time.

Window previews when you hover or put your mouse under an app's dock icon. Get window previews in the dock on MacOS, automatically open the App Exposé view in the dock today with this Mac App.

Introducing Dock Expose, an app that gives a preview of your open applications. Dock Expose opens Apple's expose view in mission control. It does so automatically whenever you put your mouse under an application's dock's icon.

Dock Exposé 4.00.1

  • Rebuilt for modern macOS, with accumulated bug fixes.
  • Close windows or hold Option to minimize them from Mission Control and App Exposé.
  • See window titles beside exposed windows and click a title to focus it.
  • Use the custom App Exposé bottom bar and press fn+R to open the Recents Editor.

Permissions and setup

Dock Exposé requires Accessibility and Screen Recording access. Full Disk Access is optional and is needed to read and edit recent documents in the Recents Editor. Input Monitoring is not required.
